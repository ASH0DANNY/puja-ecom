================================================================================
                            HOW TO GET STARTED
================================================================================

STEP 1: Create Firestore Collections (5 minutes)
  1. Firebase Console → Firestore Database
  2. Create collection: subscribedEmails (Auto ID)
  3. Create collection: promotions (Auto ID)
  4. Details in: FIRESTORE_COLLECTIONS_SETUP.md

STEP 2: Test Email Subscription (1 minute)
  1. Go to http://localhost:5174/
  2. Scroll to "Stay Updated" section
  3. Enter test email
  4. Click Subscribe

STEP 3: Create Campaign (2 minutes)
  1. Go to Dashboard → Promotional tab
  2. Click "New Promotion"
  3. Fill form and click Create

STEP 4: Send Campaign (1 minute)
  1. Click send button (paper plane icon)
  2. See success notification

STEP 5: Verify in Firestore (1 minute)
  1. Check subscribedEmails collection
  2. Check promotions collection
